Good to know

Questions, answered.

Who is the camp for?

Curious young innovators and builders aged 10–19. No prior experience is needed.

Can my children choose different formats?

Yes. Choose online or in-person separately for each child during registration.

How do I pay?

Via M-Pesa. You can trigger an instant phone prompt during registration, or pay manually via Paybill 4331541 using the Parent's Name as the Account number.

Can I register now and pay later?

Yes! Payment is completely optional at registration. You can register your children now to reserve their spot, and complete payment via Paybill 4331541 (Account: [Parent's Name]) anytime before the camp begins.
